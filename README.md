## What I build

- **Trading platform** — a live desk: charts, orders, alerts and a risk view, talking to your venue or a bot.
- **EVM dApp** — contracts, tests, a wallet-connected app and indexed data, ready for a mainnet review.
- **Solana product** — programs in Rust and Anchor, a typed client, and a consumer-facing app on Solana.
- **AI agent** — an agent or copilot that uses your docs and tools, with a clear handoff to a human.
- **Telegram mini app** — a bot or mini app that lives where your users already chat, with TON or EVM payments if you need them.
- **Website / web app** — a fast, multilingual marketing site or product frontend, deployed globally.
- **Mobile app** — an iOS and Android app — wallet, trading client or consumer product — from one codebase or native.
- **Game / GameFi** — a browser, Telegram or Unity game, including the 3D and on-chain pieces if the brief needs them.

The full catalogue — 182 technologies across 13 categories, and a line on what I can ship with each — is on [saddammalik.com/expertise](https://saddammalik.com/expertise/).
